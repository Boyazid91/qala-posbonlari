Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Qal'a Posbonlari does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No account, name, phone number or email is requested.
  • The game contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics or tracking.

  • The game does not request camera, microphone, location or contacts permissions.
  • The game is suitable for all ages, including children.
